Elder Scrolls Online Feature Work Samples

Heros Return

Problem: Players returning to the game after a long absence were often confused and overwhelmed by the amount of content and systems in the game. They often felt lost and unsure of what to do, leading to frustration and negative retention.
Solution: Designed and developed an updated tutorial and challenge campaign system tailored to returning players, with the goal of reducing friction through catch-up rewards, facilitating discovery of core gameplay loops and content, and clarifying the affordances of key game systems, features, and items. The system was built around a "learn by doing" philosophy, prioritizing hands-on engagement over passive instruction.

Path To Adventure

Problem: New and early-game players often struggle to find their footing in ESO, overwhelmed by the sheer volume of content accumulated over the years. This can lead to analysis paralysis, frustration, and ultimately, negative impacts on player retention.
Solution: Similar to Hero's Return, we designed and developed a new player onboarding experience that provides a clear "golden path" for players to follow from level 1 through 20. By completing these campaigns, players receive progress checkpoints, a defined sense of direction, and meaningful rewards that set them up for long-term success, all while learning gameplay loops that cater to a range of playstyles, from combat to exploration. The system was built around a "learn by doing" philosophy, prioritizing hands-on engagement over passive instruction.
