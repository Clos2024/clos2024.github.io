Carlos Ruiz
As a game developer, I meld my interests in gaming, technology, and storytelling to create unique experiences for people to enjoy. Playing video and tabletop games has allowed me to explore new worlds, challenge my mind, express my creativity, and make friends. I strive to create games that do all of those things and more for today's players. All I want people to remember about my portfolio of work is that I created the necessary environment for them to have fun.
Respect for the players' time, intuitive and evolving gameplay, and granting player autonomy are my core design pillars that I follow to make interesting experiences for players.
Throughout my career, I have designed and programmed games in multiple genres. I have also worked heavily with 2D/3D game engines like GameMaker, Unity, and Unreal.
Feel free to contact me with any questions you have about me or my work.
