Projects
Run N' Gun (2022
Role - Lead Gameplay Designer & Programmer
Platforms: PC
Genre: Arcade Shooter
Description: Skate and shoot your way through waves of enemies increasing your firepower along the way to become the champion. Run N' Gun is a First-person arcade shooter with a focus on movement. Become the Protagonist Bot and use your skills to survive waves of Antagonist Bots to win it all.
Solitude (2021)
Role - Solo Development Project
Platforms: PC
Genre: RTS Survival
Description: Your crew and you are stranded on a mysterious island of legend called solitude. Command your sailors to escape this mysterious island. Chop trees and mine stones to gather resources for shelter, armor, and weapons. Research how to re-construct missing components of your vessel. Cultivate land to feed your sailors. Solitude is a survival RTS game where you manage resources and battle monsters to rebuild your escape vessel.
Cave of Math (2019)
Role - Lead Gameplay Designer & Programmer
Platforms: PC
Genre: Educational Puzzle
Description: An educational math game made for the tutoring service Mathnasium. Solve math questions to escape the Cave of Math. Along the way you will be tested by various cave dwelling creatures. Test your knowledge of math arithmetic, change counting, and time telling. Play to your strengths by selecting how difficult you want your path to be.
Black Friday Blitz (2019)
Role - Lead Gameplay Designer & Programmer
Platforms: PC
Genre: Tower Defense
Description: A computer error has set all Black Friday deals to 100% off. As head of mall security, you are tasked you with defending these mismarked items against hordes and hordes of mindless consumers until Black Friday is over. Lose to many items and you'll be fired! Re-enforce your aisles with makeshift turrets to secure your job and survive Black Friday.
Swarm (2018
Role - Solo Development Project
Platforms: PC
Genre: Arcade Shooter
Description: Swarm is a 2D arcade shooter. Blast through waves of enemies and asteroids to defeat an alien force found deep in the echoes of space. Increase firepower by defeating any enemy that dare cross your path. If you can survive long enough, you may finally reach the queen and put an end to this invasive species. Battle your friends to achieve the highest score on the leaderboard.
