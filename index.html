About Me

I bring 4+ years of professional experience as a game designer, alongside years of personal projects as a self-taught programmer, building everything from small prototypes to full game systems. Gaming has given me lifelong friendships, creative fulfillment, and a lasting curiosity for technology—and with every project, I aim to give players that same sense of fun, connection, and growth.

My years of experience playing and making games shaped my core design pillars: Respect the Player's Time, Learn by Doing, and Simple Inputs: Complex Outputs. I’ve refined these through my work across genres and through insightful conversations with peers across disciplines industry-wide.
