About Me

Gaming is how I've made lifelong friends, expressed my creativity, challenged my mind, and found a passion for technology. With each project I strive to provide all those wonders for today's players by adhering to my core design pillars: respect the player's time, autonomy in play, and intuitive and evolving gameplay.

I've formed these pillars from my experiences designing and programming games in various genres — ranging from tabletop card games to MMORPGs — as well as from insightful discussions and collaborations with peers across multiple disciplines. This breadth of experience has given me both a player's empathy and a developer's practical mindset.

I would welcome the opportunity to bring my design philosophy and collaborative spirit to your team. Please feel free to review my portfolio and reach out — I'd love to discuss how I can contribute to your next project.
